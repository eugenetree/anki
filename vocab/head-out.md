# Flash Card: Head Out

## Front

Вже пізно, мені час <u>виходити</u> — побачимось завтра.
Ми вирішили <u>вирушити</u> пораніше, щоб уникнути пробок.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "It's late, I'm heading out — see you tomorrow. We decided to head out early to avoid traffic."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: head out<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to leave a place and go somewhere; commonly used in informal speech to say you are departing<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  I'm gonna head out and look for Lonnie.<br />
  We should head out before it gets dark.<br />
  She headed out early to catch the first train.
</div>
