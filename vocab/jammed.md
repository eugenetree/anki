# Flash Card: Jammed

## Front

Двері <u>заклинило</u>, і я не міг вийти з кімнати.
Я <u>прищемив</u> палець шухлядою.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The door jammed, and I couldn't get out of the room." / "I jammed my finger in the drawer."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: jammed<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: stuck or blocked so that it cannot move or work properly<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The printer jammed again and stopped printing.<br />
  The lock jammed, so I couldn't open the door.<br />
  His arm got jammed between the seats.
</div>
