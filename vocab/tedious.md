# Flash Card: Tedious

## Front

Копіювати ці дані вручну з одного файлу в інший на роботі — дуже <u>нудна</u> справа.
Довга нарада, де всі повторювали те саме по колу, здалася мені жахливо <u>нудною</u>.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Copying this data manually from one file to another at work is very tedious." / "The long meeting where everyone repeated the same things over and over felt terribly tedious to me."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: tedious<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: boring and tiring, especially because something is too long, slow, or repetitive<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Filling out the same paperwork every week at work is tedious.<br />
  The lecture was so tedious that half the class fell asleep.<br />
  Assembling the furniture piece by piece was a tedious process.
</div>
