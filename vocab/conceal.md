# Flash Card: Conceal

## Front

Він намагався <u>приховати</u> подарунок за спиною, щоб той став сюрпризом. (formal version of hide)

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "He tried to conceal the gift behind his back so it would be a surprise."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: conceal<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to deliberately hide something so it cannot be seen or found. More formal than "hide" — it often implies careful, intentional effort to keep something out of sight, and is common in writing about secrets, evidence, or objects.<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She concealed the letter inside her coat pocket.<br />
  The thieves concealed the stolen jewelry under the floorboards.<br />
  He concealed a small camera behind the painting.
</div>
