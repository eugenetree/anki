# Flash Card: Defy

## Front

Син вирішив <u>не послухатися</u> батька і все одно поїхав у подорож.
Цей трюк каскадера, здається, <u>кидає виклик</u> законам фізики.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The son decided to defy his father and went on the trip anyway. This stunt seems to defy the laws of physics."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: defy<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: (1) to openly refuse to obey a person, rule, or authority; (2) to resist or seem to go against something like gravity, logic, or explanation, especially when it seems impossible<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  There is nothing we can do without defying father.<br />
  The workers defied the ban and went on strike.<br />
  She defied her doctor's orders and went back to work early.<br />
  Protesters defied the curfew and marched through the streets.<br />
  The acrobat's leap seemed to defy gravity.<br />
  The results of the experiment defy explanation.<br />
  He defied the odds and won the tournament despite his injury.
</div>
