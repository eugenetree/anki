# Flash Card: Gracious

## Front

Навіть програвши матч, вона була дуже <u>люб'язною</u> до своїх суперників.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Even after losing the match, she was very gracious to her opponents."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: gracious<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: courteous, kind, and pleasant, especially toward someone in a weaker or lower position; showing warmth and good manners<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Our host was gracious enough to let us stay an extra night.<br />
  He accepted the criticism in a gracious manner.<br />
  She gave a gracious smile and thanked everyone for coming.
</div>
