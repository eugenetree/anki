# Flash Card: Console

## Front

Подруга намагалася <u>втішити</u> її після невдалого іспиту.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Her friend tried to console her after the failed exam."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: console (verb)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to comfort someone who is upset, grieving, or disappointed<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  He tried to console his sister after she lost the competition.<br />
  Nothing could console her when her cat went missing.<br />
  Friends gathered to console the grieving family.
</div>
