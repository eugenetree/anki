# Flash Card: Gaze

## Front

Вона довго <u>вдивлялася</u> у нічне небо, повне зірок.
Його пильний <u>погляд</u> був прикутий до картини.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "She gazed at the night sky full of stars for a long time. His intent gaze was fixed on the painting."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: gaze<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: (1) verb — to look steadily and intently at something for a long time; (2) noun — a long, steady, intent look<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She gazed out of the window, lost in thought.<br />
  He gazed into her eyes without saying a word.<br />
  There was a strange gaze in his eyes.<br />
  Under the crowd's gaze, she felt nervous.
</div>
