# Flash Card: Set Someone Up

## Front

Він не брав гроші — його <u>підставили</u>.
Вона впевнена, що її <u>підставив</u> хтось із колег.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "He didn't take the money — he was set up. She's sure one of her colleagues set her up."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: set someone up<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to make an innocent person appear guilty of a crime or wrongdoing by planting evidence, lying, or manipulating a situation<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  He didn't commit the crime — someone set him up.<br />
  The police eventually realized she had been set up by her business partner.<br />
  Don't trust him; he's the type who would set you up to save himself.
</div>
