# Flash Card: Marvel

## Front

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The Great Barrier Reef is a true marvel of nature."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: marvel (noun)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: a person or thing that makes people feel wonder because it is so impressive (in skill, size, or beauty).<br /><br />
  <strong>Marvel vs. wonder vs. miracle</strong>:<br />
  Marvel — something so impressive that it amazes people, often because of great human skill or the huge scale of nature; e.g. "a marvel of engineering."<br />
  Wonder — a lighter, more general feeling of amazement, or the thing that causes it; it doesn't have to be a big "achievement," just beautiful or surprising; e.g. "the wonders of the ocean."<br />
  Miracle — something so lucky or impossible-seeming that normal explanations don't seem enough; often connected to God or a higher power; e.g. "it's a miracle she survived."<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The pyramids are considered one of the great marvels of the ancient world.<br />
  This new phone is a marvel of modern technology.<br />
  Scientists are still amazed by this marvel of natural engineering — the spider's web.
</div>
