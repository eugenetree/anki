# Flash Card: Intimidate

## Front

Новий начальник намагався <u>залякати</u> співробітників, щоб вони не сперечалися з ним.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The new boss tried to intimidate the employees so they wouldn't argue with him."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: intimidate<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to frighten or threaten someone, often in order to make them do what you want or to undermine their confidence. Different from "scare": scare is a sudden, often brief fright (e.g. a loud noise scares you), while intimidate is usually deliberate and about asserting power or dominance over someone to control or unsettle them<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The tall players tried to intimidate their opponents before the game.<br />
  She refused to be intimidated by his aggressive tone.<br />
  Bullies often intimidate weaker kids to get what they want.
</div>
