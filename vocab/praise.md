# Flash Card: Praise

## Front

Вчителька <u>похвалила</u> його за чудову роботу.
Він не звик до <u>похвали</u> і не знав, як реагувати.
На зборах директор <u>відзначив</u> її внесок у проєкт.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The teacher praised him for his excellent work. He wasn't used to praise and didn't know how to react. At the meeting, the director praised her contribution to the project."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: praise<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: 1. (verb) to express strong approval or admiration for someone; 2. (noun) words or expressions of admiration and approval; 3. (passive: be praised) to be the recipient of such approval<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The coach praised the team for their hard work during training.<br />
  She was praised by her colleagues for handling the situation so calmly.<br />
  He rarely receives praise, but when he does, it means a lot to him.<br />
  The film received widespread praise from critics.
</div>
