# Flash Card: Foreseeable

## Front

У <u>найближчому осяжному майбутньому</u> компанія не планує змін.
Керівництво не врахувало всі <u>передбачувані</u> ризики проєкту.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "In the foreseeable future, the company doesn't plan any changes. The management didn't account for all the foreseeable risks of the project."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: foreseeable<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: able to be known or predicted before it happens. Most often used in the fixed phrase "the foreseeable future" (as far ahead in time as can reasonably be predicted), but also used more generally, e.g. "foreseeable risks/consequences"<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  We have no plans to move in the foreseeable future.<br />
  For the foreseeable future, all meetings will be held online.<br />
  The company failed to prepare for the foreseeable consequences of the merger.<br />
  Any foreseeable risks should be listed in the safety report.
</div>
