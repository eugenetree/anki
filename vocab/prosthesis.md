# Flash Card: Prosthesis

## Front

Після операції лікарі підібрали йому <u>протез</u> ноги.
Сучасні <u>протези</u> рук дозволяють виконувати майже всі повсякденні рухи.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "After the surgery, doctors fitted him with a leg prosthesis." / "Modern arm prostheses allow performing almost all everyday movements."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: prosthesis (plural: prostheses)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: an artificial device that replaces a missing part of the body, such as a limb<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  He learned to walk again with the help of a prosthesis.<br />
  Insurance covered the cost of his new leg prosthesis.<br />
  Her hand prosthesis is controlled by tiny sensors in her muscles.
</div>
