# Flash Card: Contemplate

## Front

Вона довго <u>роздумувала</u> над тим, чи варто міняти роботу.
Він сів і <u>обмірковував</u> своє рішення, перш ніж відповісти.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "She thought long and hard about whether it was worth changing jobs. He sat and contemplated his decision before answering."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: contemplate<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to think carefully and seriously about something for a period of time<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She sat quietly, contemplating her next move.<br />
  He contemplated the problem for hours before finding a solution.<br />
  They contemplated the risks before signing the contract.
