# Flash Card: Strike a Nerve

## Front

Його коментар, мабуть, <u>зачепив за живе</u> — вона одразу замовкла.
Не знаю, чому він так образився; схоже, я <u>торкнулася болючої теми</u>.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "His comment must have struck a nerve — she went quiet immediately. I don't know why he got so upset; I guess I struck a nerve."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: strike a nerve<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to say or do something that upsets, offends, or provokes a strong emotional reaction in someone, often by touching on a sensitive topic<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Her question about his job clearly struck a nerve — he left the room.<br />
  The ad struck a nerve with viewers who had lost family members to the disease.<br />
  I didn't mean to upset him; I must have struck a nerve without realizing it.
</div>
