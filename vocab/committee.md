# Flash Card: Committee

## Front

Усі важливі рішення в компанії ухвалює спеціальний <u>комітет</u>.
Батьки організували <u>комітет</u> для збору коштів на ремонт школи.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "All important decisions in the company are made by a special committee." / "The parents organized a committee to raise funds for the school renovation."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: committee<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: a group of people appointed or elected to perform a specific function, make decisions, or oversee an activity on behalf of a larger group<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The committee will meet next week to review the proposal.<br />
  She was elected chair of the finance committee.<br />
  A small committee was formed to plan the annual conference.
</div>
