# Flash Card: How Come

## Front

<u>Чому це</u> ти вчора не прийшов на зустріч? (informal for "why")
<u>Як так вийшло, що</u> вона дізналася про сюрприз раніше за всіх? (informal for "why")

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "How come you didn't come to the meeting yesterday? How come she found out about the surprise before everyone else?"<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: how come<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: an informal way of asking "why," often expressing surprise or curiosity; unlike "why," it is followed by normal subject-verb word order, not inverted<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  How come you're still awake at this hour?<br />
  How come nobody told me about the party?<br />
  If you like her so much, how come you never called her?
</div>
