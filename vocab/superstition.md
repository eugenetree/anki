# Flash Card: Superstition

## Front

Моя бабуся вірить, що чорний кіт, який перебігає дорогу, приносить нещастя — це просто <u>забобон</u>.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "My grandmother believes that a black cat crossing the road brings bad luck — it's just a superstition."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: superstition<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: a widely held but irrational belief in supernatural influence, especially that certain actions or objects bring good or bad luck<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  It's a common superstition that breaking a mirror brings seven years of bad luck.<br />
  He refuses to walk under ladders because of an old superstition.<br />
  Many sailors held superstitions about whistling on a ship.
</div>
