# Flash Card: Hiccup

## Front

У плані виникла невелика <u>заминка</u>, але ми швидко це владнали.
Технічний <u>збій</u> затримав запуск проекту на кілька годин.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "There was a small hiccup in the plan, but we quickly resolved it." / "A technical hiccup delayed the project launch by a few hours."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: hiccup<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: a minor, temporary problem or delay that doesn't cause serious or lasting damage<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  We hit a small hiccup during the software update, but it was fixed quickly.<br />
  The wedding went smoothly except for one hiccup with the catering.<br />
  Despite a few hiccups, the product launch was a success.
</div>
