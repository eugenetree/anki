# Flash Card: Call In

## Front

Сусід побачив дим і одразу <u>повідомив про це</u> пожежникам.
Робітники помітили витік нафти й негайно <u>зателефонували, щоб повідомити</u> про аварію.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The neighbor saw smoke and immediately called it in to the firefighters. The workers noticed an oil leak and immediately called it in."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: call in / call it in<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to report something (an incident, emergency, or problem) by telephone, usually to authorities or a dispatcher, so that they can respond<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  A passerby spotted the accident and called it in right away.<br />
  The oil team called it in about 18 hours after the leak started.<br />
  If you see anything suspicious, call it in to the police.
</div>
