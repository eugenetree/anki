# Flash Card: Enchant

## Front

Її ніжний спів <u>зачарував</u> усю публіку в залі.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Her gentle singing enchanted the whole audience in the hall."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: enchant<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to fill someone with great delight, charming or captivating them<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The old city's narrow streets enchanted every visitor.<br />
  She was enchanted by the beauty of the mountain view.<br />
  His storytelling enchanted the children for hours.
</div>
