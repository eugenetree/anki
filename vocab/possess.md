# Flash Card: Possess

## Front

Ця країна, за офіційними даними, <u>володіє</u> ядерною зброєю.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "This country, according to official data, possesses nuclear weapons."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: possess<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to have or own something — an object, quality, or ability. More formal than "have," often used for qualities, skills, or valuable/serious things.<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Several countries possess nuclear weapons.<br />
  He possesses a deep knowledge of ancient history.<br />
  Not everyone possesses the patience needed for this job.
</div>
