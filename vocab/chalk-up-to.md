# Flash Card: Chalk Something Up To Something

## Front

Я <u>приписав</u> його погану поведінку стресу на роботі.
Вона <u>списала</u> свою помилку на відсутність досвіду.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "I chalked his bad behavior up to stress at work. She chalked her mistake up to lack of experience."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: chalk something up to something<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to attribute or ascribe something (usually a result, behavior, or event) to a particular cause or reason; to consider something as being caused by something else<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  We can chalk his success up to hard work and dedication.<br />
  I chalked her silence up to shyness at first.<br />
  They chalked the team's poor performance up to bad weather conditions.<br />
  Don't chalk every failure up to bad luck; sometimes we need to learn from our mistakes.
</div>
