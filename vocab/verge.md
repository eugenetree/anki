# Flash Card: On the Verge Of

## Front

Компанія була <u>на межі</u> банкрутства, коли інвестор врятував ситуацію.
Я <u>ось-ось</u> засну.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The company was on the verge of bankruptcy when an investor saved the situation. I'm on the verge of falling asleep."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: on the verge of<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: very close to a particular state or about to experience something, usually something significant<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She was on the verge of tears during the speech.<br />
  Scientists say the species is on the verge of extinction.<br />
  He was on the verge of quitting his job before the promotion came through.<br /><br />
  <strong>Note</strong>: Don't confuse with "edge" (the physical border of something) vs. "verge" (the border between two states, especially "about to happen").<br />
  edge → Sit on the edge of the bed. (Сядь на край ліжка.)<br />
  verge → I'm on the verge of falling asleep. (Я ось-ось засну.)
</div>
