# Flash Card: Arrogance

## Front

Його <u>високомірність</u> дратувала всіх колег.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "His arrogance annoyed all his colleagues."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: arrogance<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: an exaggerated sense of one's own importance or abilities, often shown by a superior or dismissive attitude toward others<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Her arrogance made it hard for her to admit mistakes.<br />
  The manager's arrogance alienated the entire team.<br />
  Despite his success, he never let arrogance affect how he treated people.
</div>
