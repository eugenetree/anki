# Flash Card: Speak Up

## Front

Я не чую тебе, будь ласка, <u>говори голосніше</u>.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "I can't hear you, please speak up."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: speak up<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to speak louder so that people can hear you better. This phrase can be neutral or slightly direct/commanding depending on context. When used with "please" or in a friendly tone, it sounds polite. Without "please" or in a firm tone, it can sound more like a command or instruction.<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Could you speak up? I can barely hear you.<br />
  The teacher asked the student to speak up during the presentation.<br />
  Speak up if you have any questions about the project.
</div>
