# Flash Card: Grasp at Straws

## Front

Він висував дедалі безглуздіші аргументи — усі бачили, що він <u>хапається за соломинку</u>.
Вона продовжувала дзвонити йому, сподіваючись, що він передумає. Друзі казали, що вона <u>хапається за соломинку</u>.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "He kept making more and more ridiculous arguments — everyone could see he was grasping at straws. She kept calling him, hoping he'd change his mind. Her friends said she was grasping at straws."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: grasp at straws (also: clutch at straws)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to desperately try any possible solution or cling to any hope, however unlikely, because the situation seems hopeless<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The lawyer's arguments got weaker and weaker — he was clearly grasping at straws.<br />
  After every treatment failed, the doctors were grasping at straws.<br />
  Stop grasping at straws and accept that the project isn't going to work.
</div>
