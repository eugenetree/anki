# Flash Card: Outdo

## Front

Він завжди намагається <u>перевершити</u> своїх колег на роботі.
Ти нарешті <u>перевершив</u> самого себе цим проєктом.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "He always tries to outdo his colleagues at work." / "You've finally outdone yourself with this project."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: outdo<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to do something better than someone else, or better than you have done before<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  You've finally outdone yourself with this cake!<br />
  She always tries to outdo her sister in every competition.<br />
  The new smartphone outdoes all its rivals in camera quality.
