# Flash Card: Think Something Over

## Front

Дай мені кілька днів — мені треба <u>добре обдумати</u> цю пропозицію.
Вона не відповіла одразу: сказала, що <u>зважить</u> усе і дасть знати.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Give me a few days — I need to think this offer over. She didn't answer right away: she said she'd think it over and let them know."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: think something over<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to take time to carefully consider something before making a decision. The focus is on pausing and deliberating rather than acting immediately.<br /><br />
  Note: <em>think something over</em> emphasizes taking time before deciding; <em>think something through</em> emphasizes examining all steps, details, or consequences thoroughly. You think something <strong>over</strong> before you commit; you think something <strong>through</strong> to make sure nothing is missed.<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  I'll think it over and give you my answer tomorrow.<br />
  She thought the offer over for a week before accepting.<br />
  Can you give me some time to think this over? It's a big decision.
</div>
