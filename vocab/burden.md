# Flash Card: Burden

## Front

Піклування про хворих батьків стало для неї важким <u>тягарем</u>.
Він не хотів бути <u>тягарем</u> для своєї сім'ї.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Caring for her sick parents became a heavy burden for her. He didn't want to be a burden on his family."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: burden<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: a heavy load, or a difficult responsibility/duty that causes worry or hardship<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Debt can be a huge financial burden.<br />
  He didn't want to be a burden on his family.<br />
  The new tax rules place an extra burden on small businesses.
</div>
