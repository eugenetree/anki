# Flash Card: Pile

## Front

На столі лежала велика <u>купа</u> книжок.
<u>Купа</u> каміння блокувала дорогу.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "There was a large pile of books on the table."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: pile<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: a number of things that have been placed on top of each other; a heap or stack of objects<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She left a pile of dirty clothes on the floor.<br />
  The workers made a pile of bricks near the construction site.<br />
  I have a huge pile of homework to finish this weekend.<br />
  He sorted through the pile of papers on his desk.<br />
  There was a pile of rocks blocking the road.
</div>
