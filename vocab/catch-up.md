# Flash Card: Catch Up

## Front

Ми не бачились кілька років, тож зустрілися за кавою, щоб <u>надолужити спілкування</u>.
Після відпустки мені треба <u>надолужити</u> купу непрочитаних імейлів.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "We hadn't seen each other for years, so we met over coffee to catch up. After my vacation, I need to catch up on a pile of unread emails."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: catch up (with someone) / catch up on (something)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: (1) catch up with someone — to meet and talk after not seeing each other for a while, telling each other your news; (2) catch up on something — to do the things you fell behind on, so you're up to date again (work, sleep, news, etc.).<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Let's catch up soon — I want to hear how the new job is going.<br />
  I called my old friend to catch up on everything that happened last year.<br />
  I'm exhausted, I really need to catch up on sleep this weekend.<br />
  She spent Sunday catching up on the news she missed during the trip.
</div>
