# Flash Card: Forklift

## Front

На складі використовують <u>погрузчик</u> для переміщення важких коробок.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "They use a forklift at the warehouse to move heavy boxes."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: forklift<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: a vehicle with two metal bars (forks) at the front for lifting and moving heavy objects, commonly used in warehouses and factories<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The forklift operator moved the pallets to the loading dock.<br />
  You need special training to operate a forklift safely.<br />
  The warehouse bought a new electric forklift last month.
</div>
