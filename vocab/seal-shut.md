# Flash Card: Seal (Shut)

## Front

Не забудь щільно <u>закрити</u> пакет, щоб печиво не зачерствіло.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Don't forget to seal the bag tightly so the cookies don't go stale."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: seal (shut)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to close something tightly or airtight so nothing can get in or out<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Seal the container to keep the food fresh.<br />
  She sealed the envelope before mailing it.<br />
  The workers sealed the windows to keep out the cold air.
</div>
