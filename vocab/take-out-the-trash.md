# Flash Card: Take Out The Trash

## Front

Перш ніж лягати спати, не забудь <u>винести сміття</u>.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Before going to bed, don't forget to take out the trash."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: take out the trash<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to carry the household garbage out of the house, usually to a bin or dumpster, for collection or disposal (standard American English; "garbage" works as a synonym, British English uses "take out the rubbish")<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Can you take out the trash before we leave?<br />
  It's your turn to take out the trash tonight.<br />
  He always forgets to take out the trash on collection day.
</div>
