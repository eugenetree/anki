# Flash Card: Make Up For

## Front

Ти врятував мене, щоб <u>спокутувати</u> те, що не врятував її.
Він зробив це заради неї, намагаючись <u>компенсувати</u> свою минулу помилку.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "You saved me to make up for not saving her. He did it for her, trying to make up for his past mistake."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: make up for<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to compensate for something lost, missed, or wrong; to do something that corrects or balances a negative situation<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She worked extra hours to make up for the days she was sick.<br />
  No amount of money can make up for losing a loved one.<br />
  He baked a cake to make up for forgetting their anniversary.
</div>
