# Flash Card: Restrained

## Front

Він відреагував <u>стримано</u>, хоча був дуже роздратований.
Двоє охоронців <u>утримували</u> його, поки не приїхала поліція.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "He reacted in a restrained way, even though he was very irritated. Two security guards restrained him until the police arrived."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: restrained<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: 1. controlled and calm, not showing strong emotions or excess — describing a measured, unemotional reaction; 2. physically held back or prevented from moving freely<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She gave a restrained smile and said nothing.<br />
  The security officers restrained the protester before he reached the stage.<br />
  His tone was restrained and professional, despite the harsh criticism.<br />
  The dog had to be restrained on a leash near the busy road.
</div>
