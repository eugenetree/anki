# Flash Card: Ought

## Front

Тобі <u>слід</u> поговорити з нею про це.
Нам <u>слід було</u> приїхати раніше.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "You ought to talk to her about this. We ought to have arrived earlier."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: ought<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: used to indicate duty, obligation, or correctness, typically when criticizing someone's actions or giving advice; similar to "should" but slightly more formal<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  You ought to see a doctor if you're not feeling well.<br />
  He ought to apologize for what he said.<br />
  They ought to have been more careful with their money.<br />
  Students ought to respect their teachers.
</div>
