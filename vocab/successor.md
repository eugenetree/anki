# Flash Card: Successor

## Front

Після виходу на пенсію директора компанія обрала нового <u>наступника</u>.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "After the director retired, the company chose a new successor."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: successor<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: a person who takes over a role, position, or title from someone else<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She was named as her father's successor at the family business.<br />
  The board is still searching for a successor to the retiring CEO.<br />
  His successor took office immediately after the election.
</div>
