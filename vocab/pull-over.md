# Flash Card: Pull Over

## Front

Поліція наказала водію <u>з'їхати на узбіччя</u> і зупинитися.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The police ordered the driver to pull over and stop."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: pull over<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to steer a vehicle to the side of the road and stop, either voluntarily or when ordered to by police<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The officer signaled for him to pull over.<br />
  She pulled over to check the map.<br />
  I feel like you're gonna pull over and snuff me.
</div>
