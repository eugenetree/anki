# Flash Card: Mess Around

## Front

Припини <u>дуріти</u> і сідай вчитися.
Він цілий вечір <u>бавився</u> зі старим радіо, намагаючись його полагодити.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Stop messing around and sit down to study. He spent the whole evening messing around with an old radio, trying to fix it."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: mess around<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: 1. to waste time doing nothing useful or behaving in a silly, unfocused way; 2. to play or experiment casually with something without a clear goal<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Stop messing around — we need to finish this before noon.<br />
  The kids were messing around in the backyard all afternoon.<br />
  He likes to mess around with old car engines on weekends.<br />
  She was just messing around with the settings and accidentally deleted everything.
</div>
