# Flash Card: Get Off on the Wrong/Right Foot

## Front

Ми <u>погано почали</u> знайомство, бо я забув її ім'я на першій зустрічі.
Ми <u>вдало почали</u> співпрацю, відразу порозумівшись з новим клієнтом.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "We got off on the wrong foot getting to know each other because I forgot her name at our first meeting." / "We got off on the right foot with the collaboration, hitting it off with the new client right away."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: get off on the wrong foot / get off on the right foot<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: (1) get off on the wrong foot — to start a relationship, job, or situation badly; (2) get off on the right foot — to start a relationship, job, or situation well<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  I got off on the wrong foot with my new boss by showing up late on my first day.<br />
  Let's try to get off on the right foot this time and be honest with each other.<br />
  Their partnership got off on the wrong foot after a misunderstanding about payment.<br />
  A firm handshake and a smile can help you get off on the right foot in an interview.
</div>
