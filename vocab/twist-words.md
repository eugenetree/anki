# Flash Card: Twist Someone's Words

## Front

Не <u>перекручуй мої слова</u>, я цього не казав!

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Don't twist my words, I didn't say that!"<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: twist someone's words<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to deliberately change or distort the meaning of what someone said, often to make them look bad or to suit your own purpose<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Stop twisting my words - I never said I didn't like your idea.<br />
  The reporter twisted his words to make the story more dramatic.<br />
  Don't twist my words, I said we should wait, not that we shouldn't do it at all.
</div>
