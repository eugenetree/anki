# Flash Card: Imperfect

## Front

Це <u>недосконалий</u> світ, але це єдиний, який у нас є.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "It's an imperfect world, but it's the only one we've got."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: imperfect<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: having faults or defects; not perfect<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Her English is still imperfect, but she communicates well.<br />
  We live in an imperfect world full of imperfect people.<br />
  He learned to accept his imperfect body over time.
</div>
