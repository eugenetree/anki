# Flash Card: Unhinged

## Front

Після того скандалу він поводився зовсім <u>не при собі</u> — кричав на всіх підряд.
Її повідомлення були такими дикими, що друзі вирішили, що вона <u>збожеволіла</u>.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "After that scandal he was acting completely unhinged — yelling at everyone. Her messages were so wild that her friends decided she had gone unhinged."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: unhinged<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: mentally unstable or behaving in a wild, irrational, out-of-control way; often used informally to describe someone who has lost their composure or sanity<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  He went completely unhinged after losing his job.<br />
  Her unhinged rant on social media shocked everyone.<br />
  The character in the film becomes unhinged after years of isolation.
</div>
