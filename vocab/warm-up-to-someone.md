# Flash Card: Warm Up To Someone

## Front

Спочатку вона була насторожена, але з часом <u>потеплішала</u> до нового колеги.
Собака довго <u>не довіряв</u> гостям, та зрештою почав до них <u>тепліше ставитися</u>.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "At first she was cautious, but over time she warmed up to her new colleague. The dog didn't trust guests for a long time, but eventually started warming up to them."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: warm up to (someone)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to gradually begin to like, trust, or feel more comfortable and friendly with a person, after initially being distant or unsure about them<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  It took a while, but the new intern is finally warming up to her teammates.<br />
  He seemed cold at first, yet he warmed up to us after a few conversations.<br />
  The kids were shy around their new babysitter but slowly warmed up to her.
</div>
