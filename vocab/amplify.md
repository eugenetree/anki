# Flash Card: Amplify

## Front

Мікрофон допомагає <u>підсилити</u> голос співака на великій сцені.
Соціальні мережі часто <u>підсилюють</u> паніку серед людей під час кризи.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The microphone helps amplify the singer's voice on the big stage. Social media often amplifies panic among people during a crisis."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: amplify<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: (1) to make sound or a signal louder or stronger, usually with a device; (2) to make a feeling, effect, or message bigger, stronger, or more noticeable.<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The band used amplifiers to amplify the guitar sound during the concert.<br />
  Fear can amplify a small problem into something overwhelming.<br />
  The report's findings were amplified by extensive media coverage.<br />
  Turn up the amp to amplify the bass a little more.
</div>
