# Flash Card: Accountable

## Front

Керівник команди має бути <u>відповідальним</u> за результати роботи своїх підлеглих.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "A team leader must be accountable for the results of their subordinates' work."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: accountable<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: required to explain or take responsibility for one's actions; answerable to someone for something. Different from "responsible": responsible describes having a duty or being in charge of something (often before the outcome), while accountable emphasizes having to answer for the results afterward, usually to someone with authority (e.g. a boss, a board)<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Employees should be held accountable for their mistakes.<br />
  As CEO, she is accountable to the board of directors.<br />
  We need a system where everyone is accountable for their tasks.
</div>
