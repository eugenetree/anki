# Flash Card: Foresee

## Front

Ніхто не міг <u>передбачити</u>, що криза триватиме так довго.
Досвідчений керівник зазвичай здатен <u>передбачити</u> проблеми ще до їх появи.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "No one could foresee that the crisis would last so long." / "An experienced manager is usually able to foresee problems before they even appear."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: foresee (foresee – foresaw – foreseen, irregular verb)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to know or realize that something will happen in the future, usually based on judgment, experience, or awareness of a situation<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  She foresaw the difficulties before the project even started.<br />
  Nobody could have foreseen such a sudden change in the market.<br />
  Experts foresee a shortage of skilled workers in the coming years.<br /><br />
  <strong>Foresee vs. predict</strong>: Foresee means to sense or realize in advance that something will happen, often based on insight, experience, or reading a situation — it's more intuitive and doesn't require hard evidence (e.g. "I foresaw trouble in their relationship"). Predict means to state in advance what will happen, typically based on data, evidence, or specific reasoning — it's more formal and often measurable (e.g. "Scientists predict a 2°C rise in temperature"). In short: foresee is about perceiving, predict is about forecasting.
</div>
