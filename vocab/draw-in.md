# Flash Card: Draw In

## Front

Захисник спеціально <u>притягнув</u> суперників до себе, щоб звільнити простір для партнера по команді.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The defender deliberately drew the opponents in to free up space for his teammate."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: draw in<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to entice or lure people (or opponents) to move toward you, often as a tactic<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  You go up top, I'll draw them in and pass it back to you.<br />
  The striker drew in two defenders before laying the ball off.<br />
  The scam artist drew in victims with promises of easy money.
</div>
