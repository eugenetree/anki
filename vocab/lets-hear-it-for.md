# Flash Card: Let's Hear It For

## Front

Перш ніж він вийшов на сцену, ведучий вигукнув: "<u>Поаплодуємо</u> нашому переможцю!"
Ведучий підняв руку і закричав у натовп: "<u>Хай живе</u> капітан Америка!"

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Before he walked on stage, the host shouted: 'Let's hear it for our winner!' The host raised his hand and shouted to the crowd: 'Let's hear it for Captain America!'"<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: let's hear it for (someone)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: an expression used to encourage an audience to applaud or cheer for someone, showing appreciation or celebrating their achievement<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Let's hear it for the graduating class of 2026!<br />
  And now, let's hear it for the band that made tonight possible.<br />
  Let's hear it for all the volunteers who helped organize this event.
</div>
