# Flash Card: Rid

## Front

Влада прагне <u>звільнити</u> країну від корупції.
Лікарі сподіваються <u>звільнити</u> пацієнта від цієї хвороби.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The authorities aim to rid the country of corruption." / "Doctors hope to rid the patient of this illness."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: rid (rid – rid – rid, irregular verb)<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to make someone or something free of something unpleasant, unwanted, or harmful, usually in the form "rid X of Y"<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  The new filter helps rid the water of harmful bacteria.<br />
  She wanted to rid herself of all the stress from her old job.<br />
  The city launched a campaign to rid the streets of litter.
</div>
