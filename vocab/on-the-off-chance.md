# Flash Card: On the Off Chance

## Front

Я зайшов до крамниці — <u>раптом пощастить</u> і ще є потрібний розмір.
Він написав їй <u>просто так, раптом</u> вона ще пам'ятає його номер.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "I stopped by the shop on the off chance they still had my size. He wrote to her on the off chance she still remembered his number."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: on the off chance<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: hoping that something unlikely might happen; doing something despite a low probability of success<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  I checked the store on the off chance they had my size.<br />
  She called him on the off chance he was still in town.<br />
  He applied for the job on the off chance they were still hiring.
</div>
