# Flash Card: Pick on

## Front

Перестань <u>чіплятися до</u> мене, я не зробив нічого поганого!

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "Stop picking on me, I didn't do anything bad!"<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: pick on<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to repeatedly criticize, tease, or bully someone, often someone weaker<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  Why do you always pick on him?<br />
  Kids can be cruel and pick on each other at school.<br />
  Leave your brother alone and stop picking on him.
</div>
