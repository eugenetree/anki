# Flash Card: Testify

## Front

Свідок мав <u>свідчити</u> в суді проти обвинуваченого.
Наступного тижня експерт буде <u>свідчити</u> перед комітетом сенату.

## Back

<div id="back">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "The witness had to testify in court against the defendant." / "Next week, the expert will testify before the Senate committee."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: testify<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: to formally state facts or give evidence as a witness, usually under oath, especially in a court of law or official hearing<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  He testified that he saw the car speeding through the intersection.<br />
  The doctor was called to testify about the victim's injuries.<br />
  She refused to testify against her former business partner.
</div>
