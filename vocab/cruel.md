# Flash Card: Cruel

## Front

Він був дуже <u>жорстоким</u> до тварин, і всі це помічали.

## Back

<div style="text-align: left;">
  <strong>Translation</strong>: "He was very cruel to animals, and everyone noticed it."<br /><br />
  <strong>Word</strong>: cruel<br /><br />
  <strong>Explanation</strong>: willing to cause pain or suffering to others; showing no mercy or kindness<br /><br />
  <strong>Examples</strong>:<br />
  It was cruel of him to laugh at her mistake.<br />
  The story describes a cruel king who treated his people badly.<br />
  Fate can sometimes be cruel.
</div>
